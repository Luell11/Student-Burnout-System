YOU DON'T HAVE TO DO IT ALONE

Talk to a Trusted Adult

If something is bothering you or feels too difficult to handle, tell a trusted adult. This could be your parent, guardian, teacher, school counselor, or another adult you trust.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent or Guardian Someone at home who can listen and help.
👩‍🏫 Teacher A teacher can help you find the right support.
🧑‍⚕️ School Counselor A school support person you can talk to.
🧑‍🦳 Another Trusted Adult Someone responsible who makes you feel safe.
